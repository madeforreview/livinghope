Our history

Started in living rooms. Still learning to depend on God.

Living Hope began in 1997 with a few small groups meeting in homes. Our address has changed several times since. Our heartbeat hasn't.

  1. 1997

    Small groups in homes

    Living Hope Community Church began as a handful of small groups meeting in living rooms across Halifax, with Jesus Christ and dependence on Him at the centre.

  2. The following years

    A movie theatre and storefronts

    As the church grew, we gathered wherever God opened a door, including a movie theatre and a series of storefronts. Each move taught us to trust Him to make the way clear.

  3. Today

    Halifax Christian Academy

    We now partner with Halifax Christian Academy and meet each Sunday at 114 Downs Ave in Fairmount.

Part of a bigger family

Living Hope is a member of the Canadian National Baptist Convention, a diverse fellowship of churches across Canada that values the autonomy of each local church while working together in missions and evangelism.

Our prayer for this church

Jesus' prayer in John 17 has become our own: that we would be one in Him, and that people would recognise Christ in the way we care for one another.

"that they may all be one; even as you, Father, are in me, and I in you, that they also may be one in us; that the world may believe that you sent me."
John 17:21 (WEB)
