Our pastor

Pastor Ian MacFarlane

Raised in Halifax, sent around the world, and called home to serve the people of this city.

Ian & Joy MacFarlaneFamily photo coming soon

Halifax is home

Ian was born in Moncton, New Brunswick. When he was three, his family moved to Halifax, and he has been a Haligonian ever since. It's the only home he has known, and the city he loves to serve.

From the offshore to the pulpit

Before ministry, Ian worked as a data engineer in the offshore oil industry. Over time, God grew in him a desire for Christian education and full-time ministry. He graduated from Trinity Bible College and Southern Seminary, earning a Master of Divinity.

A missionary heart

Ian has served as a missionary in Australia. Even far from home, Halifax stayed close to his heart, and today he pours that missionary spirit into our own neighbourhood.

Family

Ian is married to Joy, who serves our children through Kids' Zone and Upward Sports. They have a daughter, Nerida, and a son, William.

Ian continues to discover the abundant life of Christ and longs to see Christ living through, and for, His Church.
