Your first Sunday

What to expect

  1. Before 10:30

    Arrive and settle in

    Come a few minutes early. Someone will welcome you, help you find a seat, and show you where the nursery is if you need it.

  2. 10:30 AM

    Worship together

    We sing contemporary songs, with the occasional hymn, and pray together. Join in as much or as little as you like.

  3. 11:15 AM

    Kids head to Kids' Zone

    Children ages 5 to 11 go to junior church for Bible stories, music, games and crafts while adults stay for the message.

  4. The message

    Teaching you can live

    Bible teaching centred on Jesus, honest about real life, and practical for the week ahead.

  5. Afterwards

    Stay and chat

    No rush to leave. It's a good time to ask questions and meet people. Watch for our monthly luncheons, too.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Call 902-445-5908 or email info@livinghopehalifax.com.

What time is the Sunday service?

Our worship service starts at 10:30 AM every Sunday at Halifax Christian Academy, 114 Downs Ave, Halifax. The livestream on our Facebook page starts at the same time.

Where exactly do you meet?

We meet at Halifax Christian Academy, 114 Downs Ave, in the Fairmount subdivision off Joseph Howe Drive. From Joseph Howe Drive, turn up Springvale Avenue.

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. Our services are relaxed, and you'll see everything from jeans to Sunday best.

What is the worship style?

Contemporary worship songs, with traditional hymns from time to time, followed by Bible-based teaching focused on Jesus Christ.

Is there something for my kids?

Yes. Nursery care is available for infants and toddlers. Children ages 5 to 11 can join Kids' Zone, our junior church, from 11:15 AM to 12:00 PM with music, Bible stories, games, videos, crafts and snacks.

Can I watch online?

Yes. Our Sunday service streams live on Facebook starting at 10:30 AM.

What kind of church is Living Hope?

Living Hope Community Church is an evangelical Baptist church and a member of the Canadian National Baptist Convention. We have been part of Halifax since 1997.

Do I have to be a Christian to attend?

Not at all. Whether you follow Jesus, are exploring faith, or are simply looking for community, you're welcome.

Optional

Let us know you're coming

Tell us you're planning to visit and we'll look out for you, save you a seat, and answer any questions ahead of time. You never need to register to attend.
