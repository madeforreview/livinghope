Upward Sports

Basketball where every kid gets to play

Upward Sports teaches teamwork, respect, sportsmanship and character in a Christian environment. All playing abilities are welcome.

Why parents love Upward

Upward is about more than basketball. Kids learn to work as a team, respect their coaches and referees, and grow in confidence, while caring adults model the love of Jesus.

Fair play, built into every game

Every child plays about the same amount of time. Our substitution system gives every player a chance to start, makes sure no one sits out two periods in a row, and has every child on the court for at least half of each game.

Volunteer with us

Upward runs on volunteers. You can pray for the program, coach, referee, keep score or time, or lead halftime activities. Training is provided. Coaches complete a police record check and coach training.
