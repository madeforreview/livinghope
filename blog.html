Pastor's Page

Who are you? Really! Who are you?

By Pastor Ian MacFarlane · A reflection on Colossians 1:26–27

In this reflection

  • Why appearances never tell the whole story
  • What we mean by "the abiding life"
  • "Christ in you, the hope of glory"
  • Gravity, flight and the law of sin and death
  • An invitation to stop striving
Talk with Pastor Ian

Most of us know one another only on the surface. We recognise a face, a job title, a way of dressing. But is that who we really are? This reflection begins with that simple, searching question.

The abiding life

At Living Hope we often talk about "the abiding life." You might also hear it called the dependent life, the life of rest, the life of Christ, or the Spirit-filled life. Each name points to the same reality: a life lived in daily dependence on Jesus.

Christ in you, the hope of glory

"…the mystery which has been hidden for ages and generations. But now it has been revealed to his saints… which is Christ in you, the hope of glory." Colossians 1:26–27 (WEB)

Pastor Ian explains this "hope of glory" as the hope that God's own character will be expressed through an ordinary person. We were created in God's image. Through His death and resurrection, Christ restores that image in us.

How a plane overcomes gravity

Gravity never switches off, yet aircraft fly every day. A greater law, aerodynamics, lifts them above it. In the same way, the indwelling Spirit of Christ lifts us above "the law of sin and death," giving real peace even in hard circumstances.

Will you?

The reflection closes with an invitation: stop trying to live the Christian life in your own strength, and start trusting and depending on Christ, who lives in you. Will you?

This page summarises Pastor Ian's article. If you'd like to talk about any of it, he'd be glad to hear from you.
