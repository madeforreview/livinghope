New here?

What a Sunday at Living Hope feels like

Walking into a new church takes courage. Here's what you can expect, so nothing catches you off guard.

Relaxed and real

Our gatherings are comfortable and unhurried. Wear what you like. You'll be greeted, not singled out.

Worship centred on Jesus

Contemporary songs with the occasional traditional hymn, prayer, and Bible teaching you can bring into Monday.

Your kids, well cared for

Nursery for infants and toddlers, and Kids' Zone for ages 5–11 with music, Bible stories, games, crafts and snacks.

Loving on our city

Faith that shows up on Halifax streets

We believe the love of Jesus should be practical. These are a few of the ways our church family serves our neighbours.

Givers Way Street Ministry

We meet people experiencing homelessness in Halifax with backpacks of personal-care items and blankets, a meal-time conversation, and the story of Christ.

Handy-Man Ministry

Volunteers help neighbours with basic home repairs. A fixed door or a working tap can say "you matter" louder than words.

Upward Sports

A Christian basketball program where every child plays, learns teamwork and sportsmanship, and is encouraged by caring coaches.

H2O Youth: Hope to Others

A place for teens to connect with each other and with God through activities, snacks and honest discussion.

Meet our pastor

A Haligonian who came home to serve

Pastor Ian MacFarlane grew up in Halifax, worked as a data engineer in the offshore oil industry, served as a missionary in Australia, and came back to the only city he has called home.

He holds a Master of Divinity from Southern Seminary and is married to Joy, who leads our Kids' Zone. His heart is to see the life of Christ lived out through His church, right here in our neighbourhood.
