What we believe

Our statement of faith

We are an evangelical Baptist church. These convictions shape everything we do, and at the centre of them all is Jesus Christ.

In short

Salvation is God's free gift, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.

  • The Scriptures

    All Scripture is inspired by God, and the original manuscripts are without error. The Bible is our final authority for faith and life.

    2 Timothy 3:16–17

  • One God in three persons

    There is one eternal God, existing in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

  • The virgin birth

    Jesus Christ was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary. He is both Son of Man and Son of God.

  • The resurrection of Jesus

    Jesus rose bodily from the dead, ascended into heaven, and now intercedes for believers as our High Priest and Advocate.

  • Created in God's image

    God created humanity in His own image. Sin has separated every one of us from Him.

  • Atonement for sin

    Christ died as the sacrifice for our sins. Through Him, God offers forgiveness as a free gift of grace.

  • Salvation by grace

    Salvation is received by faith in Jesus Christ, not earned by works. All who trust Him are born again by the Holy Spirit and become children of God.

  • The Holy Spirit

    The Holy Spirit is essential to the Christian life, empowering holy living, producing spiritual fruit, and equipping believers to serve.

  • The Christian walk

    Followers of Jesus are called to belong to, identify with, and serve through a local church as we fulfil the Great Commission together.

  • Jesus is coming back

    Jesus Christ will return personally and bodily to establish His kingdom.

  • Resurrection and judgment

    Both the just and the unjust will be raised bodily: believers to everlasting blessing, and unbelievers to everlasting conscious punishment.

  • The Great Commission

    Christians are called to make disciples until Christ returns, and the church should actively support world evangelism.

Have questions about any of these? We'd love to talk. Get in touch.
