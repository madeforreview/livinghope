Bible resources

Tools for reading the Bible every day

These are the free resources our church family turns to for daily devotion and deeper study.

Read and search the Bible

  • Bible Gateway

    Read and search Scripture in many languages and translations. Look up passages by keyword, phrase or reference.

  • Blue Letter Bible

    Free study tools including commentaries, concordances and original-language helps.

  • Christian Classics Ethereal Library

    Hundreds of classic Christian books, free to read online.

Daily devotionals

  • My Utmost for His Highest

    Oswald Chambers' much-loved daily devotional.

  • F. B. Meyer: Our Daily Walk

    Daily devotional readings from F. B. Meyer.

  • A. B. Simpson: Days of Heaven on Earth

    A year of daily readings from the founder of the Christian and Missionary Alliance.
